Artist Update

JUNE 2023

Updates re: 2023 and plans for the rest of the year

2023

Hope this finds you in good spirits. It's time for a quick update since my last deck in February - so, welcome to the second edition of 2023!

Some exciting things have happened - I managed to pull off my first solo exhibition (big yay!), created multiple dynamic artworks, got to share my thoughts at a bunch of cool events, and saw my work showcased across the globe. All in all, it's been a whirlwind of creativity, learning, and some seriously late nights.
Inside this deck, you'll find more details on my recent adventures and a sneak peek into what's coming next.

Thank you for being part of this wild ride. Here's to more art, more breakthroughs, and more shared success!

Solo Exhibition

Embracing the nuances of the human journey, my solo exhibition plunged into a vibrant exploration of self, time, and the spectrum of human experiences. Open for two weeks, it attracted 500+ visitors, ranging from seasoned tradtional and NFT art collectors to newcomers to the NFT space.

01.

During the exhibition, seven unique digital creations were unveiled, including six 1/1 pieces and one editioned artwork. Various technological mediums such as screens, projections, and AR were employed to showcase these works. The integration of technology not only enhanced the viewing experience but also holistically amplified the underlying concepts of the artworks.

Out of the six unique pieces, four found new homes. Additionally, the editioned artwork was well-received, with 144 editions being snapped up by eager collectors.

Read about the dynamic artworks in detail here.

The exhibition garnered attention from a diverse range of media outlets. It was highlighted in publications such as Vogue Singapore and Tatler Asia, as well as Time Out and NFT Culture, among others.

02.

Throughout the exhibition, I conducted both public and private artist led tours and helped organise insightful panel discussions. These sessions centered around important topics such as intellectual property and copyright within the digital art landscape, cultivating a space for reflective conversations and collective knowledge sharing.

03.

Speaking Engagements

One of the highlights of my recent journey has been the chance to share my perspective at various NFT-focused events. These engagements have allowed me to take on the role of a speaker, delving into the intricacies of being a digital artist in the NFT realm, and fostering conversations around the future of this exciting space.

NFT KOREA - I had the pleasure to engage in a panel discussion about transitioning from traditional to digital art realms, was invited by Playnomm after a successful sale with them.

Mar 2023

Digital Paradise, Sanya, China - the focus was on the Chinese art market and its potential for NFTs. It was an exciting opportunity to strengthen my presence and share insights into the expanding NFT ecosystem.

Apr 2023

HSBC Next Gen Digital Panel - This panel was tailored towards the Next Gen Ultra High Net Worth individuals, concentrating on digital assets. It was intriguing to understand their perspective on the NFT space and present a fresh viewpoint.

Apr 2023

NFT Bali - The event was attended by local artists from Bali, Indonesia. It provided a chance to share the work we do at NFT Asia and recount our journey in building this vibrant community.

May 2023

NFC Lisbon - NFC Lisbon attracted artists and art enthusiasts from all corners of the globe. During my talk, I had the opportunity to share insights on organizing my own solo exhibition and provide practical tips for artists who aspired to do the same. It was a rewarding experience to connect with fellow artists and inspire them on their creative journeys.
