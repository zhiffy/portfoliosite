June 2023 Update

A first solo exhibition, work shown around the world, and a stretch of talks, features, and seriously late nights.

Shavonne Wong
June 2023Singapore, SG
Opening note

Hope this finds you in good spirits. It’s time for a quick update since my last deck in February, so welcome to the second edition of 2023. Some exciting things have happened: I pulled off my first solo exhibition, created multiple dynamic artworks, shared my thoughts at a bunch of events, and saw my work shown across the globe.

All in all, it’s been a whirlwind of creativity, learning, and some seriously late nights. Thank you for being part of this wild ride. Here’s to more art, more breakthroughs, and more shared success.

Solo Exhibition
01 / 032023

The Ties That Bind

UltraSuperNew Gallery, Singapore

Embracing the nuances of the human journey, my first solo exhibition plunged into a vibrant exploration of self, time, and the spectrum of human experience. Open for two weeks, it drew 500+ visitors, from seasoned traditional and NFT collectors to newcomers to the space.

During the exhibition, seven unique digital creations were unveiled, six 1/1 pieces and one editioned artwork, shown across screens, projections, and AR. The technology not only enhanced the viewing experience but amplified the underlying concepts of the works. Four of the six unique pieces found new homes, and the edition was well received, with 144 editions snapped up. Read about the dynamic artworks in detail here.

02 / 032023

In the press

The exhibition drew attention from a diverse range of media outlets, highlighted in Vogue Singapore, Tatler Asia, Time Out, and NFT Culture, among others.

03 / 032023

Tours and panels

Throughout the exhibition I led both public and private artist tours and helped organise panel discussions. These sessions centred on important topics such as intellectual property and copyright in the digital art landscape, cultivating a space for reflective conversation and shared knowledge.

Global Showcases
—2023

Shown around the world

Over the past few months my artworks have journeyed far and wide, from the Beijing Contemporary Art Fair to streets in Milan, the art scene in New York, and exhibitions in Tokyo, among others.

Exhibited at W1 Curates × Canary Labs (London), 6060 at the NFC Summit (Lisbon), Reaching for the Future at UltraSuperNew Gallery (Tokyo), Beijing Contemporary at Neal Digital Gallery (Beijing), Digital Paradise at Neal Digital Gallery (Sanya), The Ties That Bind at UltraSuperNew Gallery (Singapore, solo), and the NFT Korea Festival (Seoul).

Speaking Engagements

One of the highlights of the past few months has been sharing my perspective at NFT-focused events, taking on the role of a speaker, digging into what it means to be a digital artist in this space, and fostering conversations about where it’s heading.

01 / 05Mar 2023

NFT Korea Festival

Seoul, Korea

I joined a panel on transitioning from traditional to digital art, invited by PlayNomm after a successful sale with them.

02 / 05Apr 2023

Digital Paradise

Sanya, China

The focus was the Chinese art market and its potential for NFTs, an exciting chance to strengthen my presence and share insights into the expanding NFT ecosystem.

03 / 05Apr 2023

HSBC Next Gen Digital Panel

Next Generation 2023

Tailored to next-generation ultra-high-net-worth individuals and focused on digital assets, it was intriguing to understand their perspective on the NFT space and offer a fresh viewpoint.

04 / 05May 2023

NFT Bali

Bali, Indonesia

Attended by local artists from Bali, this was a chance to share the work we do at NFT Asia and recount our journey building this vibrant community.

05 / 05Jun 2023

NFC Lisbon

Lisbon, Portugal

NFC Lisbon drew artists and enthusiasts from all corners of the globe. In my talk I shared how I organised my own solo exhibition and offered practical tips for artists hoping to do the same. A rewarding experience to connect with and inspire fellow artists.
