Overview

Created with photographer Lenne Chai, By Proxy follows an imaginary young girl's transition from girlhood to adolescence. The central figure is digitally sculpted as an amalgamation of the artists' younger selves and their shared upbringing in Singapore.

The series blends photography and 3D virtual modeling, deliberately blurring what was captured, what was constructed, and what was remembered into being.

The story

The project grew from a natural merging of two practices: Wong's work in 3D virtual modeling and NFTs, and Chai's emotionally raw photographic language. Together they created scenes that feel personal and surreal, rooted in the slippery textures of childhood memory.

At the heart of the project is a virtual teenage girl moving through a world shaped by memory. The ambiguity is intentional: moments bleed into each other, reality overlaps with imagination, and nostalgia becomes a kind of reconstruction.
