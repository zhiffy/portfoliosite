CGI and photography series · 2022

By Proxy.

A 60-piece collaborative series by Shavonne Wong and Lenne Chai, dropped on Quantum Art in April 2022.

Can memories, just like images, be manipulated?

Featured works →Complete series →OpenSea collection →Back to works

Overview

Created with photographer and director Lenne Chai, By Proxy follows an imaginary young girl's transition from girlhood to adolescence. The central figure is digitally sculpted as an amalgamation of the artists' younger selves and their shared upbringing in Singapore.

The series blends Wong's 3D virtual modeling and NFT practice with Chai's evocative photographic language. Together, they built a body of images that deliberately blurs what was captured in-camera, what was constructed in CGI, and what was remembered into being.

    Process

    The project merged Wong's virtual modeling with Chai's photographic sensibility. The protagonist was sculpted digitally, then placed into scenes that use the visual grammar of photography: gesture, light, staging, and familiarity. The result is neither documentary nor pure fantasy, but a hybrid memory system.

    Statement

    By Proxy asks whether childhood is remembered as it happened or rebuilt by the adult self into something that feels true. The series uses a digitally constructed proxy not to falsify memory, but to show how memory already behaves like an image: edited, staged, softened, and sometimes invented.

    Supporting texts

    The series sits in conversation with coming-of-age photography, family archives, and digital identity. It uses the NFT format as provenance for sixty discrete works while keeping the emotional grammar closer to memory, friendship, and image-making than to collectible mechanics.
