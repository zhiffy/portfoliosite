05 · Sentience beyond the human

Plants, animals, and the possibility that awareness was never ours alone. Also how differently other things hold time.

Greenland sharks, and how a bird moves when you slow it down

30 May 2026 · Note

A bird pecking looks random until you slow it down, and then it looks thoughtful. Plants look like they are dancing when you speed them up. Greenland sharks process time slowly in the deep cold and live a long while, so a long life must feel even longer to them.

An awe piece, not a guilt piece

25 April 2026 · Note

If people can feel awe at the possibility that a plant is aware, that changes the relationship to the world more than guilt does. I do not know yet what it looks like.
