Artist Update

JAN 2023

Updates re: 2022 and plans for Q1 and Q2 of 2023!

2022

Welcome to my digital deck, where I take you on a journey through my art and my experience in the NFT space! 2022 was an unforgettable year for me, filled with exciting highs and valuable lessons. I delved deeper into the industry, honed my craft, and learned a lot about human behavior and myself. I had the honor of being showcased at the prestigious Venice Biennale and even had the opportunity to sell my works on Sotheby's. Let's take a look at what I've been up to and what's to come!

2023

It's only the beginning of the year, but I've already been making moves! I've had a successful drop on Playnomm, selling a piece to the Korean market for a whopping 21 ETH. I've also had the pleasure of exhibiting at Proof of Concept and ART SG, and collaborated with Cryptocubes by HanRGB on a drop. To top it off, I was chosen to be a part of the TIME's X Deepak Chopra drop, designing a cover and creating a 3D space with Artifex. It's shaping up to be an exciting year and I can't wait to see what else is in store!

Studio Updates

Happy to share that I've been pushing my limits and expanding my skillset! I've been honing my 3D techniques to achieve even greater realism in my work. This year, I'm excited to focus on creating dynamic and interactive pieces that truly push the boundaries of what's possible. I'm exploring new ways to make my art more immersive and engaging for the viewer. I want to challenge traditional notions of art and push the boundaries of what's possible. As for AI, I'm interested in experimenting with using AI algorithms to generate unique textures and patterns, or using AI-powered tools to enhance the realism of my 3D models. I believe that AI has the potential to take my art to the next level and open up new possibilities for creativity. It's all about constantly improving and pushing myself to be the best artist I can be!
