January 2023 Update

A look back at 2022, from the Venice Biennale to Sotheby’s, and a glimpse of what’s already in motion for 2023.

Shavonne Wong
January 2023Singapore, SG
Opening note

Welcome to my digital deck, where I take you on a journey through my art and my experience in the NFT space. 2022 was an unforgettable year, filled with exciting highs and valuable lessons. I went deeper into the industry, honed my craft, and learned a lot about human behaviour and myself. I had the honour of being shown at the Venice Biennale and even sold work on Sotheby’s. Let’s look at what I’ve been up to, and what’s to come.

Into 2023
—Early 2023

A fast start to the year

It’s only the beginning of the year, but I’ve already been making moves. I had a successful drop on PlayNomm, selling a piece to the Korean market for 21 ETH. I exhibited at Proof of Concept and ART SG, and collaborated with Cryptocubes by HanRGB on a drop. To top it off, I was chosen for TIME’s x Deepak Chopra drop, designing a cover and creating a 3D space with Artifex. It’s shaping up to be an exciting year.

In the studio
—2023

Pushing the work further

I’ve been honing my 3D techniques for even greater realism. This year I’m focused on dynamic, interactive pieces that push what’s possible and make the work more immersive and engaging, challenging traditional notions of art along the way.

I’m also interested in experimenting with AI, using it to generate unique textures and patterns and to enhance the realism of my 3D models. I think it has real potential to open up new possibilities. It’s all about constantly improving and pushing myself to be the best artist I can be.

What’s ahead

A few things are already in progress, including an installation in build and more showings of recent work.
