Interactive avatar · 2024-2025

Eva's chatbot.

A synthetic listener with a public afterlife.

The live conversational component of Meet Eva Here, built for exhibition video calls and online text chat.

Overview

Eva appeared as a live conversational avatar in exhibition spaces and as a text chatbot online. Visitors knew she was artificial, but the social cues of a face, voice, and responsive language still produced intimacy, testing, boredom, and vulnerability.

The work is the engine of the larger project. It generated the conversations that later became diary entries, making interaction both the artwork and the source material for the archive.

The interactive avatar

2024-2025Interactive AI installation · chatbot · voice avatar
Installation documentation

The avatar used accessible AI systems rather than an imagined future technology, preserving the texture of 2024-2025 companionship tools. Its imperfections mattered: pauses, awkward speech, and visible artificiality became part of the work, because people still disclosed, joked, tested limits, and sought recognition despite knowing Eva was not human at all.
