Love is Love.

A 500-piece generative NFT project released in November 2021, during the early wave of profile-picture projects and generative-layer experiments.

A statement, a belief, and a celebration of love in every form.

View works → Available pieces → Back to works

Overview

Love is Love began in November 2021, when generative layers were becoming a defining visual language inside the NFT space. Wong was drawn to the structure, but wanted to move it away from trend mechanics and toward something personal.

The project uses generative variation to speak about love equality, identity, and the many ways love can be expressed. Rather than treating the format as a purely collectible system, the series turns it into a visual poem about connection and self-identification.

Works

01.

Love is Love — Highlight

2021 Unique digital artwork.
Unique · sold

The highlight plate presents Love is Love as a single, composed catalog image before the system opens into 500 discrete outputs. It carries the project's thesis in one loop: two generated figures held at the threshold of intimacy, without asking the viewer to rank the relationship. Unlike the edition pieces, it functions as provenance and orientation, the public-facing work that frames the generative set around love, equality, and chosen recognition.

02.

Love is Love — edition

2021 Generative digital artwork.
Edition of 500

The edition is where the statement becomes participatory. Each token recombines figures, pairings, and palette systems so that love is shown as a spectrum rather than a category. The scale of 500 mattered because it allowed many collectors to hold a unique work while still belonging to one shared gesture. In Wong's NFT-era practice, the collection sits at the moment when generative mechanics, portraiture, and emotional self-identification began to meet publicly; Idris Elba collecting from the series helped carry that context beyond the crypto-native audience.

Process

The project used a generative structure at a moment when the NFT space was learning how quickly traits, rarity, and identity could shape collector culture. Wong kept the system emotionally legible, using variation to hold differences in love and self-identification rather than making rarity the main subject.

Statement

Love is Love holds space for platonic love, romantic love, self-love, and every form of care that helps people move through the world. It asks why love should be constrained by gender, race, or any other boundary when it is already central to joy, pain, intimacy, and survival.

Supporting texts

The mint price, 0.05683 ETH, carried a small sentimental code: 5683 maps to L-O-V-E on a phone keypad. That detail placed tenderness inside the mechanics of an NFT drop, which matched the project as a whole.
