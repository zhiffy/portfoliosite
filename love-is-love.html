Project. 2021

Love is Love.

A statement, a belief, and a celebration of love in every form.

2021 . 500-piece edition + 1 highlight . Generative digital artwork . Ethereum

A 500-piece generative NFT project released in November 2021. The system recombines figures and palette systems so love appears as a spectrum rather than a category, holding space for platonic, romantic, and self-love alongside every other form of care. The edition has been collected widely, including by Idris Elba.

Project text

In the heart of the NFT wave in November 2021, amid the frenzy of pfp projects, Shavonne Wong found an opening for artistic expression through generative layers. Fascinated by the technology but not fully convinced by its dominant use, she shaped it toward something personal: a system that could echo her beliefs, sentiments, and sense of care.

Love is Love became more than a series of generative artworks. It was made as a statement, a belief, and a celebration of love in all its forms and colors; a way of speaking for love equality while humanizing the abstract language of generative art.

The details carried that tenderness into the mechanics of the drop. The mint price, 0.05683 ETH, was chosen because 5683 maps to L-O-V-E on an old phone keypad.

The project sold out within an hour and reached collectors beyond the crypto-native audience, including Idris Elba, who purchased three Love is Love NFTs as his first-ever NFTs.

The project also marked Wong's first foray into generative art, opening a path toward using art and technology together as a form of visual poetry.

A note. the highlight

Alongside the 500, one unique highlight.

The Highlight plate (sold) carries the project’s thesis in a single composed work: two generated figures held at the threshold of intimacy, without asking the viewer to rank the relationship. It functions as provenance and orientation, the public-facing image that frames the generative set around love, equality, and chosen recognition.

The edition’s mint price, 0.05683 ETH, carried a small sentimental code. The number 5683 maps to L-O-V-E on a phone keypad.

Statement

Love, whether platonic, romantic, or self-love, is an intrinsic part of human experience. So much joy and pain stems from love, and the work asks why anyone should have their experience denied or be told that the love they have is wrong.

Life can already be painful and the world can be harsh. Regardless of gender, race, or anything else, the project insists on a simple grace: people should be able to choose who they go through it with. Love is love, to anyone who finds it.
