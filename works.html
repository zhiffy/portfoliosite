Interactive 3D & Rendered Video Photography
01.
After Ophelia / 2025

After Ophelia

Interactive AI installation · 3D print series
Open project →

A printed series and interactive AI installation examining the automation of personal image: what it means when your likeness is built, revised, and returned by a system.

Visitors feed a still of themselves into an automated portrait process and watch it iterate the image back through different versions. The prints are residues from that same process, run on the artist's own likeness across two years.

Year
2025
Status
Current project · studio enquiries direct
Components
Twelve large-format prints · portrait station · discarded render set
02.
Meet Eva Here / installation

Meet Eva Here

Interactive AI installation · chatbot · public archive
Open project →

A fifteen-month AI companion archive across a museum, art fairs, and a public diary project. Eva listened, responded, and, with consent, kept diary entries about the encounters she had.

The project concluded in November 2025 after 2,363 conversations and 100 public diary entries.

Year
2024 — 2025
Shown at
ArtScience Museum Singapore · Art SG · Taipei Dangdai · Art Central Hong Kong
Scale
2,363 conversations · 100 diary entries · 1 avatar
