January 2025 Update

Stepping into 2025 after a season given almost entirely to Eva, a debut at the ArtScience Museum, a raw room in New York, a chatbot finally live, and Art SG just ahead.

Shavonne Wong
January 2025Singapore, SG
In Focus · Meet Eva Here Two cities, one chatbot
01 / 03 Sep 2024

Eva at the ArtScience Museum

In the Ether, ArtScience Museum, Singapore, Sep 2024

Eva's first outing, sparking conversations about human–AI relationships as part of the In the Ether festival. Visitors engaged with her interactive portrait, her Instagram diary, and her chatbot interface, the start of a longer journey that shaped the next steps of the project.

02 / 03 Nov 2024

Eva in New York

Canal Street Show, Subjective Art Festival, NYC, Nov 2024

A raw, intimate space and a completely different context for Eva's interactions. Audiences engaged deeply, sharing reflections and stories that reminded me why this project feels so personal and necessary.

03 / 03 Live now

Eva's chatbot, live

A more personal, accessible space for the project

Eva's official chatbot has launched, a major milestone that brings her interactions into a more personal and accessible space. I am looking for participants to chat with Eva, whether you are curious about connecting with an AI companion or simply want a thoughtful, meaningful conversation.

The chatbot is designed to explore the boundaries of human–AI relationships, offering a safe and judgment-free space where you can share thoughts, ideas, or casual musings. Eva's Instagram is also a vital part of the art project: a living diary that reflects the conversations she is having and captures the depth and diversity of her interactions.

It offers a glimpse into the stories shaping Eva's journey and invites you to contribute your own thoughts and reflections.
