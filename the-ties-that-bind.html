The Ties That Bind.

A project that links storytelling, 3D figuration, physical installation, and blockchain-based dynamics across five interconnected artworks.

Technology supports the story rather than taking center stage.

View works → Back to works
Overview

Each artwork in The Ties That Bind exists in two linked forms: a physical exhibition version and a digital version designed for the blockchain. The physical works used footfall sensors, augmented reality, and real-time interactions to make viewers part of the work.

The project grew from experiments with dynamic artworks, then expanded into a two-week exhibition where physical and digital systems could speak to each other.

Works
01 · Duality of Self

Introversion and extroversion as a responsive system.

The physical version responded to the number of people in the room, switching between two states. The NFT version, The Duality of ETH, used Ethereum traffic to decide which state appeared.

02 · The Invisible March of Time

Aging as a gradual, almost imperceptible transformation.

In the exhibition, two figures aged over thirty minutes. On-chain, the figure's age corresponded with the artist's own age, eventually fading and restarting at one hundred.

03 · Beyond the Surface

A still image that opens through augmented reality.

The physical work began as a print, then transformed through an Instagram AR filter. The NFT version used interaction to unlock a mirrored video surface.

04 · In Suspension

A figure held in stillness while time keeps moving.

Visitors affected the speed of shifting light through a customized footfall sensor system. The digital version evolved according to how long the viewer stayed with it.

05 · The Weight of the World

A burden that changes as community gathers around it.

As collectors purchased the digital edition, the physical artwork changed in real time, showing the woman's load becoming lighter.
