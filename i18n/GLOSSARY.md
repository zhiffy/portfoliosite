# Translation glossary (frozen terms)

Source of truth for what does and does not get translated on any localized
page. Added 2026-06-19 with the language-system pilot. Applies to every locale
(zh-Hans, zh-Hant, fr, th) and to any future language.

## Never translate (keep in English / Latin script)

These stay in English in every language. Translating them harms searchability
and reads as amateur.

- **Artist name:** Shavonne Wong. A one-time gloss in-script is allowed the
  first time on a page (e.g. Shavonne Wong（黄）), never a full re-spelling.
- **Her work and project titles:** Meet Eva Here, EVA, After Ophelia,
  Ophelia Retold, Ophelia Reassembled, By Proxy, The Ties That Bind,
  Whirlwind of the Waking Dream, Echoes of Identity, The Bubble We Call Home,
  Love is Love, Conditional, The Waiting Room, The Mirror.
- **Exhibition and show titles:** keep as written (e.g. The Times of Chimeras,
  Infinite Games: Hello World!, Talking to Machines).
- **Venue and institution names:** ArtScience Museum, Venice Biennale, ART SG,
  Taipei Dangdai, Art Central, Paris Photo, The Columns Gallery, NFT Factory,
  Bangkok Art and Culture Centre, and the rest. City names may be localized
  inside prose (e.g. 新加坡, 曼谷) but venue names stay in English.
- **Awards:** Forbes 30 Under 30 Asia, Prestige 40 Under 40, TIMEPieces.
- **Press outlets:** The Straits Times, Vogue, Tatler, CoinDesk, Bloomberg,
  Artnet, Grazia, Her World, and similar.
- **Brands and collaborators:** Shu Uemura, Bang & Olufsen, Vogue Singapore,
  Marie Claire Arabia, World Economic Forum, The Fabricant.
- **Communities:** BLOOM, NFT Asia.
- **Studio email and URLs.**

## Always translate

- Section headings and UI labels (nav, buttons, captions).
- Biography and artist-statement prose.
- Work descriptions and the "In brief" question-and-answer block (high value
  for non-English answer engines).
- Image alt text (describe what the image shows, in the target language; keep
  brand and work names per the rule above). Pre-venue Conditional images stay
  "concept render", not "installation view", in every language.
- Role and context words around proper nouns (e.g. Solo, Panelist, Speaker,
  Performance lecture).

## Notes

- Classic external works may use their established target-language title
  (e.g. Hamlet renders as 《哈姆雷特》 in Chinese). This applies only to
  third-party canonical works, never to Shavonne's own titles.
- Keep numbers, years, and measured facts identical across languages. A single
  source of truth per fact; if the English changes, the translations follow.
- Do not invent venues, dates, or claims in any language. Translate only what
  the English source states.
