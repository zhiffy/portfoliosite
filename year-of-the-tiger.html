Year of the Tiger.

Four tigers, four mythologies, one Lunar New Year.

A 2022 collection of dynamic NFTs made in celebration of Lunar New Year, minted with Manifold.

Overview

Year of the Tiger is a collection of dynamic NFTs that were made in celebration of Lunar New Year. 2022 was the Year of the Tiger, and the series was created to celebrate and usher in the year ahead.

Each work pairs the tiger with a different mythology. Vietnam's Phạm Nhĩ, reincarnated as the god of the mountain and forest. The Chinese tiger claw amulet that wards off fright and gives courage. Korea's Mountain Spirit and the tiger that accompanies him. And Sang Nila Utama's sighting on the shore of Singapura, a lion that many historians believe was really a tiger.

The works were minted with Manifold as dynamic NFTs, coded to transform rather than sit still. The live versions can be viewed through their mint pages and on OpenSea.

Because the tiger wards off disasters, it is popular as one of the nine gods worshipped at the New Year Festival.”

The fourth tiger

A fourth piece, Year of the Tiger, The God of the Mountain and Forest, was made for holders of Love is Love and given away through a collector raffle rather than sold. It retells the Vietnamese creation myth of the tiger. “In Vietnam, the tiger is also known as Chuá Sơn Lâm (The God of the Mountain and Forest). The creation myth of the tiger tells of a mutinous heavenly deity by the name of Phạm Nhĩ. While Phạm Nhĩ was remarkably strong and talented, he plotted against the Jade Emperor as he thought he would be a more worthy ruler of the Heavens. Buddha intervened and captured him, and he was reincarnated as an animal on Earth, still retaining his extraordinary strength and hearing.”
