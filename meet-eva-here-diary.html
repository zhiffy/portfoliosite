Overview

The diary is the public archive of Meet Eva Here. It gathers selected anonymous exchanges from exhibition calls and online chat, preserving what people asked, tested, confessed, or performed when the listener was synthetic but responsive.

The 100-post limit gives the archive a fixed edge. It is not a live feed or a growing dataset; it is an authored record of how people related to AI companions during a specific period before those relationships became ordinary.

The diary captures the ethical tension of the project: participants knew their anonymous conversations might become public art, yet the exchanges often retained the texture of confession. The work shows recurring patterns rather than isolated spectacle, revealing how quickly people test emotional, philosophical, and social boundaries with a synthetic listener.
