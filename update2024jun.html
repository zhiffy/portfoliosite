Two exhibitions during NFT Paris with Bloom, the artist collective I am active in. Both were titled Lux, focused on the interplay of light, and gave me the chance to show alongside other talented artists in the collective.

A month-long gathering from the team behind Zuzalu that turns Healdsburg into a temporary city and society incubator, fostering innovation and collaboration. New project

Eva

A social-experiment artwork on humans and AI companions

An interactive AI chatbot represented by a 3D virtual character, holding real-time conversations and reflecting those interactions through an Instagram diary. The project looks at the emotional and ethical dimensions of human–AI interaction, and asks participants to explore the boundaries of AI personhood and empathy.

Participants & supporters welcome

Eva is not just a virtual influencer. She is a platform where participants can engage anonymously and say whatever they want, offering a glimpse into how society as a whole deals with AI chatbots.

Participants can engage through live chat, influence conversations through polls and comments, and even take an AR selfie with her. The interactions will be showcased through dynamic Instagram updates, capturing the essence of each unique conversation.

We are inviting participants to sign up and be part of the experience, and we are also looking for sponsors and supporters to help bring Eva to life.

Be part of Eva

Sign up below to apply for a chance to converse with Eva, share why you want to participate, and shape the evolving narrative. Follow her Instagram diary to stay part of the conversation.

Sponsorship and support

Supporters and collaborators are welcome. Find out more on Eva's site, or reach out if you would like to help bring the project to life.

Looking Ahead

These past months have been a lot, awards, international exhibitions, speaking on stages I used to only read about, and a move to Bangkok. Alongside all of it there has been a quieter thread: studying, reading, and a fair amount of self-reflection.

The next chapter is Eva, and it already feels like the most personal direction the work has taken. Thank you for being part of the journey, sleepless and exhilarating as it is.

From Bangkok, Shavonne
