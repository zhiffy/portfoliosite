January 2024 Update

A year of showing in New York, Paris, and Beijing, collaborations with Marie Claire and CoinDesk, and a big move to Bangkok ahead.

Shavonne Wong
January 2024Singapore, SG
Opening note

Hello and a very Happy New Year. It’s 2024 and I’m back with another burst of updates from my world of art and digital creation. Since last June’s update it’s been quite an exhilarating journey, showing my work in some of the most vibrant art spaces around the world, from the streets of New York to the art districts of Paris and Beijing.

Collaborating with names like Marie Claire Arabia and CoinDesk has been a dream, and being recognised on a few prestigious lists has been surreal. This update breaks down those milestones and gives a glimpse of what’s ahead, including a big move to Bangkok. Thank you for being part of this adventure.

Exhibitions & Showcases
01 / 03Sep 2023

Solo exhibition at NFT Factory

NFT Factory, Paris

At my NFT Factory Paris exhibition I showed my digital art journey, offering a unique window into the NFT art world. Thanks to the venue’s extensive digital displays, it was a significant milestone that let me present my work like never before.

I had the wonderful opportunity to give tours to enthusiasts and collectors, including groups from YSL Beauty, Chanel, Bang & Olufsen, and Bright Moments, sharing the stories behind my creations. It’s such a surreal feeling when people are genuinely into my art and curious about what goes into it. The experience was both humbling and exhilarating.

02 / 03Nov 2023

Sound and Vision II

Lume Studios, New York

In November 2023 I took part in the audio-visual exhibition Sound and Vision at Lume Studios. Curated by ONBD and held on 21 November, the show blended sound and visual art.

03 / 03Nov 2023

Infinite Games: Hello World!

Neal Gallery, 798 Art District, Beijing

Infinite Games: Hello World! ran at Shenzhen AI Space in Beijing’s 798 Art District from 16 November 2023 to 7 January 2024, exploring the intersection of gaming, digital possibilities, and human-computer interaction. My artwork In Suspension was featured alongside diverse pieces, highlighting how digitalisation shapes our understanding of rules and realities.

CoinDesk #MostInfluential2023

CoinDesk · Panopticon

I had the thrilling opportunity to collaborate with CoinDesk on their #MostInfluential2023 collection, creating a digital 3D portrait of Sam Altman. Straying from my usual style to capture his likeness, the work carries a thriller theme intensified by its sound design and explores the eerie concept of surveillance. Read more here.

05 / 05Nov 2023

Vogue Singapore — Voices: The Next Generation

Next In Vogue

I was invited to join the panel Voices: The Next Generation, hosted by Vogue Singapore for their Next In Vogue event. The session focused on the future of the creative industry, bringing together emerging voices in art and fashion to talk about building our businesses, refining our creative processes, and the intersection of culture, fashion, and technology. Moderated by Chandreyee Ray, with fellow speakers Shye, Qiyun Woo, and Mae Tan. A fantastic chance to share insights and learn from other creatives.

In the studio
—2024

Learning, and where the work is heading

Over the last three years I’ve been expanding my skill set in a few directions. Alongside improving my technical skills with new software and tools, I’ve recently been diving into art history and philosophy. This isn’t just about being better at what I do, but understanding the bigger picture behind my art.

My exploration of art history has been eye-opening, showing me a variety of styles and influences and helping me connect traditional art movements with modern expression. I’ve also started getting into philosophy, focusing on ideas about identity and what makes us who we are. These topics have really got me thinking and have begun to shape how I approach my work.

You might have noticed I’ve been using AI in a scene of my latest work. I’m not just playing with new tech for its own sake. I’m really interested in how it can help me express the human experience, especially as we move further into a digital world. I’m looking forward to seeing where all this learning and experimenting takes me.
