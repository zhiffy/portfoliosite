Exhibitions & Projects
01 / 03 Sep 2023

Solo Exhibition at NFT Factory

NFT Factory, Paris, Solo exhibition

September brought a truly memorable solo exhibition at NFT Factory in Paris, where my works were showcased across the venue's extensive digital displays. The show featured several of my 1/1s and offered space to share the stories behind the work with visitors, collectors, and groups from YSL Beauty, Chanel, Bang & Olufsen, and Bright Moments.

It was humbling and exhilarating in equal measure, and it was a real joy to connect with the collectors and visitors who came through.

02 / 03 Nov 2023

Sound and Vision II

Lume Studios, New York

The Shimmering Veil was included in Sound and Vision II, an audio-visual exhibition curated by ONBD at Lume Studios in New York. It was a one-night exhibition blending sound and visual art.

03 / 03 Nov 2023

Infinite Games: Hello World!

Neal Gallery, 798 Art District, Beijing

In Suspension was featured in Infinite Games: Hello World! at Neal Gallery in Beijing's 798 Art District. The exhibition explored the intersection of gaming, digital possibility, and human-computer interaction, looking at how digitalisation reshapes our sense of rules and reality.

Looking Ahead

Over the last three years I have been widening the work in a few directions, new software and techniques, yes, but lately also art history and philosophy. It is less about getting better at what I do and more about understanding the bigger picture behind it.

Art history has been genuinely eye-opening, connecting older movements with what I am trying to say now. Philosophy, especially ideas about identity and what makes us who we are, has started to shape how I approach the work. You may have noticed AI creeping into a recent piece; I am not chasing the tech for its own sake, but for how it can express the human experience as we move further into a digital world.

Your support is what fuels this exciting, sometimes sleepless journey. Thank you for being part of the adventure.

Onward, Shavonne
