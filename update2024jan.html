2024

Hello and a very Happy New Year everyone!

it’s 2024 and I'm back with another burst of updates from my world of art and digital creation! Since last June’s update, it’s been quite an exhilarating journey!
I've had the privilege of showcasing my work in some of the most vibrant art spaces around the world, from the bustling streets of NYC to the iconic art districts of Paris and Beijing. Collaborating with giants like Marie Claire Arabia and CoinDesk has been a dream come true, and being recognized on prestigious lists has been surreal.

This update will provide a breakdown of these milestones and a glimpse into what the future holds – including a big move to Bangkok! I'm thrilled to share these stories with you, hoping they resonate and inspire.

Your support means the world to me, and it's what fuels this exciting, sometimes sleepless, journey. Thank you for being part of this adventure!

Exhibitions, Art Shows, and Global Showcases

01.

At my NFT Factory Paris exhibition, I showcased my digital art journey, offering a unique window into the NFT art world. This show was a significant milestone, allowing me to explore and present my artistic journey like never before, thanks to the venue's extensive digital displays

I had the wonderful opportunity to give tours to enthusiasts and collectors, including groups from YSL Beauty, Chanel, Bang & Olufsen, and Bright Moments, sharing insights and stories behind my creations. It really is such a surreal feeling when people are genuinely into my art and curious about what goes into it.

The experience was both humbling and exhilarating, reaffirming the value of my artistic endeavors. Thank you to everyone for your continued support and for being part of this incredible journey.

Sound and Vision II, Lume Studios, NYC, USA (November 2023)

In November 2023, I participated in the audio-visual exhibition "Sound and Vision" at LUME Studio. This event, curated by ONBD and held on November 21st, blending sound and visual art.

03.

Infinite Games: Hello World!, Neal Gallery, 798 Art District, Beijing, China (November 2023).

"Infinite Games: Hello World!" was an engaging exhibition at Shenzhen AI Space, 798 Art District, Beijing, from Nov 16, 2023, to Jan 7, 2024. It explored the intersection of gaming, digital possibilities, and human-computer interaction. My artwork "In Suspension" was featured alongside diverse pieces, highlighting how digitalization shapes our understanding of rules and realities.

CoinDesk #MostInfluential2023 Collection: Created and sold 'Panopticon', a digital portrait of Sam Altman (November 2023).

I had the thrilling opportunity to collaborate with CoinDesk on their #MostInfluential2023 collection, alongside some amazing artists. My piece for this collection was a new and exciting challenge: creating a digital 3D portrayal of Sam Altman. Straying from my usual style to capture his likeness. Coindesk’s article.

The artwork carries a unique thriller/horror theme, intensified by its sound design and delves into the eerie concept of surveillance. Read more here.

Speaking Engagement at Vogue Singapore - VOICES: THE NEXT GENERATION (November 2023).

I was invited to be part of a panel discussion titled "VOICES: THE NEXT GENERATION" hosted by Vogue Singapore for their Next In Vogue event. The session focused on the future of the creative industry, bringing together four digitally savvy and emerging voices in the realms of art and fashion. We delved into topics around building our businesses, refining our creative processes, and adapting to the dynamic intersection of culture, fashion, and technology. The discussion was moderated by Chandreyee Ray and included fellow speakers Shye, Qiyun Woo, and Mae Tan.

This engagement was a fantastic opportunity to share insights and learn from fellow creatives making their mark in the digital world.

Studio Updates

Over the last three years, I've been expanding my skill set in a few different areas. Along with improving my technical skills with new software and technologies, I've also been recently diving into art history and philosophy. This isn't just about being better at what I do but also understanding the bigger picture behind my art.

My exploration into art history has been really eye-opening for myself. It's shown me a variety of styles and influences and has helped me connect traditional art movements with modern expressions.

I've also started getting into philosophy, focusing on ideas about identity and what makes us who we are. These topics have really gotten me thinking and have started to shape the way I approach my art.

You might have noticed that I've been using AI in a scene of my latest work. I'm not just playing around with new tech for the sake of it, I'm really interested in how it can help me express the human experience, especially as we move more into a digital world.

I'm really looking forward to seeing where all this learning and experimenting takes me. I'm on a journey of discovery here, and I think it's going to add a lot of new layers to what I create. Keep an eye out for what's coming next!
